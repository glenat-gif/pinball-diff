# Pinball changes: Queensland, 2026-10-16 to 2026-10-22

- **New venue:** [Wellers Hill Bowls Club](https://pinballmap.com/map?by_location_id=30761) in Tarragindi with Gladiators (Gottlieb, 1993).
- **Swap:** [BrewDog DogTap Brisbane](https://pinballmap.com/map?by_location_id=14333) in Brisbane City replaced Centigrade 37 (Gottlieb, 1977) with Centigrade 37 (Gottlieb, 1977).
- **3 new machines:** [BrewDog DogTap Brisbane](https://pinballmap.com/map?by_location_id=14333) in Brisbane City added Stars (Stern Electronics, 1978), Cosmic Princess (Allied Leisure, 1979) and Meteor (Stern Electronics, 1979). (thanks victabeer)
- **2 new machines:** [BeastieMonk](https://pinballmap.com/map?by_location_id=30027) in Ipswich added Fish Tales (Williams, 1992) and Black Jack (Bally, 1977). (thanks mischke)
- **Rotation:** [Netherworld](https://pinballmap.com/map?by_location_id=9885) in Fortitude Valley brought in Creature from the Black Lagoon (Bally, 1992) and moved out Funhouse (Williams, 1990). (thanks jimmynails)
- **3 gone:** [The Clubhouse Moorooka](https://pinballmap.com/map?by_location_id=17389) removed Cosmic Princess (Allied Leisure, 1979), Meteor (Stern Electronics, 1979) and Gladiators (Gottlieb, 1993).

Lineups confirmed unchanged at 2 venues.
